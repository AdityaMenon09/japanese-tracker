# Japanese in One Hour a Day — A Plan for Procrastinators

A free, self-guided plan to go from "I forgot my kana" to functional Japanese, on about 1 hour a day.

## Which path, and why

**Recommended path: the free self-guided route (Tae Kim + Anki), not a textbook.**

For someone who procrastinates, the two killers are startup friction and the all-or-nothing guilt spiral. This path removes both:

- **Zero startup friction** — start today, for free, no book to order and no "I'll begin once it arrives" excuse.
- **Streaks do the discipline for you** — Anki, jpdb, and WaniKani all track daily streaks. "Don't break the chain" beats willpower.
- **A built-in escape hatch** — the *Minimum Viable Day* rule (below) keeps your streak alive on lazy days so one missed session never turns into quitting.

The Genki textbook path is better for people who *like* structure and checking boxes. For a procrastinator, "sit down and study a chapter" is exactly the kind of big, vague task that gets postponed.

---

## The two rules that carry everything

**Rule 1 — Don't break the chain:** 15 minutes of flashcards, every single day. Spaced repetition only works daily. Same time, same place — treat it like brushing your teeth, not like studying.

**Rule 2 — The Minimum Viable Day:** On bad days, do *only* the flashcards. Quitting comes from the all-or-nothing trap, not laziness. Ten lazy minutes keep the streak alive and kill the guilt. That's a win, not a failure.

## Phase 0 — Wake your kana back up (Week 1–2)

Since you learned kana once, this comes back fast. Reactivate it, don't restudy from zero.

- **Days 1–5 — Hiragana:** Use Tofugu's free Learn Hiragana guide (all 46 with mnemonics in an afternoon). Then drill on Kana Pro or Real Kana until you read any character in under 2 seconds.
- **Days 6–10 — Katakana:** Same method, Tofugu's matching guide.
- **The test:** read kana aloud with no chart. When you stop pausing, you're done. Drop romaji forever after this — even if it's not perfect. You cement it through real reading later.

## Your daily hour (Month 1 onward)

| Time | Activity | Why it's there |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 15 min | Vocabulary — SRS flashcards | The non-negotiable daily habit. Do it first so it always happens. |
| 20 min | Grammar | The rules that turn a pile of words into real sentences. |
| 15 min | Kanji (inside vocab) | Learn 水 in お水 / 水曜日, never as a lonely character. |
| 10 min | Listening / immersion | Train your ear from day one, even when you understand almost nothing. |

- **Daily total:** 60 min
- **Lazy-day floor:** 15 min (flashcards only)

## The road ahead (18-month arc)

**Months 1–3 — ≈ N5**
- Grammar: work through Tae Kim's Guide in order.
- ~1,000 common words in SRS · ~100 kanji via vocab.
- Goal: read simple sentences, introduce yourself, catch basic phrases.

**Months 4–8 — ≈ N4**
- Finish Tae Kim; start reading graded readers (Tadoku Level 0).
- Vocab to ~2,000 · kanji to ~300.
- Goal: hold simple conversations, read short easy texts.

**Months 9–18 — ≈ N3**
- Shift weight to immersion — native content with lookup support.
- Grammar becomes review and gap-filling.
- Goal: understand a lot of daily content, read slowly on your own.

## Your free toolkit (every item is free)

**Kana**
- Tofugu Hiragana / Katakana guides — best free kana intro anywhere
- Kana Pro · Real Kana — fast recognition drilling

**Grammar**
- Tae Kim's Guide — complete free course, web + app
- Imabi — deep free reference for later
- Genki textbook — not free, but answer keys, audio, and drills are widely available online; worth buying if you can

**Vocab & Kanji (SRS)**
- Anki + the Kaishi 1.5k deck — the daily flashcard engine (free on desktop/Android; iOS app is paid)
- jpdb.io — free vocab + kanji with SRS and streaks
- WaniKani — first 3 levels free, great for kanji

**Listening & Reading**
- Comprehensible Japanese (YouTube) — beginner immersion
- Nihongo con Teppei — free graded podcast
- Tadoku readers (tadoku.org) · NHK Easy News — free graded reading

**Lookup**
- Jisho.org — the free Japanese–English dictionary
- Yomitan — hover-to-translate browser extension

---

## Practical advice

1. **Consistency beats intensity.** 1 hour daily crushes 7 hours on Sunday. The SRS reviews especially must be daily.
2. **Learn kanji inside vocab**, not as isolated characters. 水 alone is nearly useless; 水曜日 / お水 sticks.
3. **Start listening immediately**, even when you understand almost nothing. Your ear needs the mileage.
4. **Don't chase perfection** on kana or grammar before moving on — you cement everything through repeated exposure to real Japanese, not by mastering isolated pieces.
