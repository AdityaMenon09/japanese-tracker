_Created 12 August 2026 · exam target: **Sunday 6 December 2026** (~116 days / ~16 weeks away)_

This doc has two parts:
1. **What the app now does** — the five new exam-prep sections and how to use each.
2. **Your study plan** — how to spend ~1 hour a day to be ready for N5 by December.

## Part 1 — What's new (the changes)

Five new sections were added to `app.html`. Your existing **Quiz** and
**Flashcards** are unchanged, and the look is the same. All four new *drills*
use the real JLPT four-choice (multiple-choice) format.

Live at <https://japanese-tracker-six.vercel.app/app.html>.

| Section | Where it sits | What it's for | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **JLPT N5 countdown** | below "At a glance" | Keeps the deadline real | Just glance at it. Shows days left, the exam's 3 sections, ~90 min, and the 80/180 pass mark. |
| **Grammar drills** | after the Quiz | The whole ② grammar block the app never trained | Pick 8/12/20 questions → fill the blank → every answer explains itself. Best score is saved. |
| **Reading practice** | next to Grammar | ④⑤⑥ comprehension + finding info in a notice | "Start a passage" → read → answer. Tap **Show English** to check yourself. |
| **Vocabulary by theme** | after the Kana chart | The "basic stuff": numbers, days, colours, body, family… | Tap a theme chip, tap any word to hear it, toggle rōmaji off as you improve. 16 themes. |
| **Mock exam** | before the heatmap | A timed dry-run in real exam order, scored by section | "Start mock exam" → ~20 questions, 20-min timer → score per section vs the pass mark. |

**On your phone vs. this laptop:** audio and the mock's **listening** section only
appear where a Japanese voice exists. Your phone (and Microsoft Edge) has one;
this Windows laptop's Chrome does not, so listening is skipped there. **Do
listening practice on your phone.**

## Part 2 — Your 1-hour-a-day study plan

### The goal, in numbers

To pass N5 you roughly need:

| Target | Amount | This app covers it via |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary | ~800 words | Flashcards (SRS) + Vocabulary by theme |
| Kanji | ~100–120 | picked up inside vocab (don't drill in isolation) |
| Grammar | Minna no Nihongo I, Lessons 1–25 | Lessons section + Grammar drills |
| Reading | short passages / notices | Reading practice |
| Listening | everyday dialogues | phone audio + external links below |

Over ~110 study days that's about **8 new words a day** and **~1 kanji a day** —
very doable. Front-load vocabulary early, then shift to review + exam skills.

### The one rule

**Never break the flashcards.** Even on a dead day, do your SRS reviews — that
alone keeps the streak and keeps memory from decaying. Everything else is a bonus
on top.

### Your daily hour (the repeatable template)

| Min | Block | In the app |
|----:|---|---|
| 15 | **Flashcards / SRS** — reviews first, then ~10 new cards | Flashcards |
| 20 | **Grammar** — study 1 point, then drill it | Lessons + Grammar drills |
| 10 | **Vocabulary** — one theme, learn or review | Vocabulary by theme |
| 10 | **Listening _or_ Reading** (alternate days) | Reading practice / phone audio |
| 5 | **Warm-down** — Sentence of the day, tap to hear | Sentence of the day |

Log each block on the **Today** check-in so your streak and heatmap fill in.

### Weekly rhythm

- **Mon–Fri:** the daily hour above.
- **Saturday:** lighter — consolidate the week's themed vocab + one Reading passage.
- **Sunday:** a **Mock exam**, then spend the rest of the hour reviewing every
  question you got wrong. This is where the real learning happens.
- **Minimum day:** just flashcards. Keep the streak alive.

### The four phases to December

**Phase 1 — Foundation · ~Aug 12 – Sep 1 (Weeks 1–3)**
- Lock kana *cold*: read the Kana chart with rōmaji **off**, no hesitation.
- Build the SRS habit (10–15 new cards/day).
- Grammar: **Minna Lessons 1–5**.
- Themed vocab: **Numbers, Days, Dates, Time, Greetings, Family**.
- Milestone: kana automatic · ~150–200 words in SRS.

**Phase 2 — Core grammar build · ~Sep 1 – Oct 12 (Weeks 4–9)**
- Grammar: **Lessons 6–15** (particles, adjectives, likes/ability, existence,
  counters, comparing, wanting, the て-form). Grammar drill **every day**.
- Themed vocab: **Colours, Body, Food & drink, Places, Weather, Position,
  Adjectives, Verbs, Question words**.
- Start Reading practice; add light listening on your phone.
- Milestone: ~500 words · ~60 kanji · particles & verb/adjective forms solid.

**Phase 3 — Finish the grammar + exam skills · ~Oct 12 – Nov 9 (Weeks 10–13)**
- Grammar: **Lessons 16–25** (て-form uses, ない-form, dictionary form, plain
  style, 〜と思います, noun-modifying clauses, conditionals).
- Reading + listening **daily** now, not alternating.
- Take your first full **Mock exams** — expect a low score at first; that's fine.
- Milestone: ~750–800 words · ~100 kanji · all N5 grammar seen once.

**Phase 4 — Polish · ~Nov 9 – Dec 6 (Weeks 14–16)**
- **Mock exam 2–3× per week**, always reviewing the misses.
- Heavy review, little new material: re-run Grammar drills, re-read every Reading
  passage, sweep all 16 vocab themes with rōmaji off.
- Timed practice so 90 minutes feels normal. Do listening on your phone.
- Milestone: consistently **60%+ on mocks** — comfortably above the real 80/180.

### How to know you're on track

- The **countdown** and **heatmap** show your consistency — aim for a dark heatmap.
- Your **Grammar / Reading best scores** should climb toward ~80%.
- Your **Mock exam** best should reach **60%+** by mid-November. The real pass
  mark is 80/180 (~44%) with per-section minimums, so 60% in practice is a safe
  cushion.

### External practice (already in the app's "Free study links")

- **Listening:** Comprehensible Japanese (YouTube), Nihongo con Teppei (podcast).
- **Reading:** Tadoku graded readers (Lv 0), NHK Easy News.
- **Grammar reference:** Tae Kim's Guide, Imabi.
- **Lookup:** Jisho.org.
- Before the exam, do a couple of **official-format practice papers** to feel the
  timing (search "JLPT N5 official practice workbook").

### Two honest caveats

1. This is a *study companion*, not the whole course — pair it with your Minna no
   Nihongo book for the grammar explanations.
2. The mock's **listening** is word-level for now (not full N5 dialogues), so keep
   doing real listening on your phone / the linked channels.
